## About &#123;&#123;BRAND&#125;&#125;

&#123;&#123;BRAND&#125;&#125; is a website at [aisvg-generator.com](/) that turns text prompts and selected images into editable SVG files you can preview and download. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the website, create an account, generate artwork, purchase credits, or contact us.

For privacy questions or requests, email [support@aisvg-generator.com](mailto:support@aisvg-generator.com).

## Text Prompts, Images, and SVG Files

### Text to SVG

When you generate artwork, your text prompt and generation settings are sent to our servers and an external AI generation provider. We store the prompt, settings, task identifiers, processing status, credit usage, and generated result. Your first preview can be created without registering; that record is associated with an essential visitor session and is linked to your account when you sign in. These records support generation, billing, troubleshooting, and recovery.

The provider also processes and may retain prompts, generated files, and task records under its own policies. Its [data-handling policy](https://www.seeapi.com/docs/data-handling/) describes its processing and retention practices. Do not include passwords, API keys, confidential documents, or sensitive personal information in a prompt.

### Image to SVG

Your browser resizes the PNG, JPG, or WebP image you select, then sends a normalized copy to our servers for vector conversion. The image converter does not send your source image to an AI provider. A temporary image copy may be stored while processing is pending. After successful conversion, we retain the vector result and a raster preview to support recovery and downloads.

Before payment, the browser receives a raster preview rather than the editable SVG source. Access to a saved preview requires the original visitor session or its linked account. Downloading an editable SVG requires a verified paid purchase and any applicable credit charge.

### Free-preview limits

An essential signed visitor cookie and account records enforce the one-preview allowance. We also use keyed hashes derived from network and browser information to limit repeat claims and abuse; the preview quota records do not store raw IP addresses or raw browser identifiers. Clearing a cookie does not necessarily reset the allowance. Anonymous limits cannot reliably identify the same person across every device or network.

### Downloads and Browser Storage

When you download an SVG, the file is saved through your browser to the location you choose. We do not have access to your local download folder.

We use browser storage to remember preferences and save the request identifier, prompt, settings, and task identifier needed to resume a pending text generation or recover the latest result. These saved details may remain in that browser until they are replaced or you clear the site's storage. On a shared device, sign out and clear site data when appropriate.

## Other Information We Handle

- **Account information:** your name, email address, authentication records, and profile details you choose to provide. If an external sign-in option is offered and you use it, we receive the account information needed to sign you in.
- **Billing and credit records:** selected products, order and subscription identifiers, payment status, purchased credits, and credit usage. Payment processors handle payment details; &#123;&#123;BRAND&#125;&#125; does not store full payment card numbers.
- **Technical and usage information:** information available when you interact with the website, such as IP address, browser and device details, request times, referring pages, errors, and product events. The information collected depends on the feature and analytics tools in use.
- **Support communications:** your email address, message, relevant account or order details, and any materials you choose to send us.

We use information to operate your account, generate and deliver SVG files, maintain task progress, calculate credits, process purchases, manage subscriptions, respond to support requests, understand website performance, and investigate errors or abuse. We also keep records needed for security, accounting, and applicable legal obligations.

## Service Providers and Other Disclosures

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies and browser storage support sign-in, account security, the free-preview allowance, display preferences, and saved generation tasks. Image-conversion retries store an input fingerprint and request identifier rather than the source image. Analytics tools, where enabled, may use cookies or similar technologies to measure traffic and product usage.

Where the website displays a cookie consent control, use it to manage the choices offered. You can also manage cookies and site storage in your browser. Blocking or clearing essential storage may sign you out, reset preferences, or remove the browser's shortcut to a saved task. Clearing browser storage does not delete account or generation records held on our servers or by service providers.

## Retention and Deletion

We retain account, generation, billing, and support records for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, resolving disputes, and meeting applicable recordkeeping requirements. We do not promise that prompts or generated files are automatically deleted immediately after download. Provider-held records follow the provider's own retention practices.

To request account closure or deletion of information, contact [support@aisvg-generator.com](mailto:support@aisvg-generator.com). Include your account email and the relevant task or order identifiers, if available. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some records may need to be retained for legal, billing, fraud-prevention, or security reasons; removing a task from one system does not automatically remove every provider or backup copy.
